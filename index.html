      For our guests

      Travel & Stay

      Everything you need to reach the palace, and a little of Dubai to enjoy between the celebrations.

      • Most flights land at Dubai International Airport (DXB), about 27 km from the palace, or 30 to 35 minutes by taxi. Some flights use Al Maktoum International (DWC), about 45 minutes away. Please check the visa rules for your passport before you travel.

        View location

      • The nearest Metro station is Mall of the Emirates on the Red Line, about 3 km from the palace; taxis wait outside for the 5 to 10 minute ride. Bus 81 runs from the bus station beside the Metro to the Burj Al Arab stop, a 12-minute walk away. Pay with a Nol card and plan trips in the RTA S'hail app.

        View location

      • Official cream RTA taxis queue outside every arrivals hall, at the Metro station and at the palace entrance, day and night. Careem and Uber work across Dubai. Ask for “Jumeirah Al Qasr, Madinat Jumeirah”, or open the route below for your driver.

        Route to the palace

      • We suggest Jumeirah Beach Hotel, the wave-shaped hotel on the same beach, about five minutes from the palace. Mina A'Salam and Dar Al Masyaf at Madinat Jumeirah are even closer.

        View location

      • Our celebrations are at the palace, Jumeirah Al Qasr at Madinat Jumeirah, beside the Burj Al Arab. Taxis drop guests at the main palace entrance, and valet parking is available.

        View location

      • An evening in the red dunes: a dune drive, camel rides, sandboarding and dinner under the stars. Most tours collect you from your hotel in the afternoon and bring you back after dinner.

        View location

      • Pavilions from around the world, street food, shopping and shows. Open for the season from October to May, 4 pm to midnight (1 am at weekends), about 30 minutes from the palace.

        View location
